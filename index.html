故事The game

一个被安排好的人生，和第一次真正属于自己的朋友。A life arranged by others — and the first friends that were truly hers.

“谁替你写下了你的人生？”“Who wrote your life for you?”

阿莱格拉十六岁，在一个什么都要放在“正确位置”的家庭里长大。入学的那一天，她第一次和同龄人住进同一间屋子——一个坦率而温暖的室友，一个冷静、总坐在窗边的转学生。这是她人生里第一段真正由自己选择的关系。

然后，一些细小的东西开始“不在原来的位置”：一句过快的回答，一张半夜空着的床，一本被人翻过的书。

《代笔》是一个关于身份、记忆与共谋的故事。你要决定阿莱格拉如何去爱、如何依赖，以及为了被爱，愿意成为怎样的人。 Allegra is sixteen and raised in a household where everything has its proper place. On her first day at a strict boarding school she shares a room with someone her own age for the first time — a warm, blunt roommate, and soon a calm transfer student who always sits by the window. These are the first relationships of her life that are truly her own.

Then small things stop being where they belong: an answer given too quickly, an empty bed in the middle of the night, a book that has been moved.

Ghostwritten is a story about identity, memory and complicity. You decide how Allegra loves, how she depends on others — and what she is willing to become to be loved.

核心玩法Core ideas

你不是在选结局，你是在成为一个人。You are not choosing an ending. You are becoming someone.

I

看不见的人格A personality you can’t see

四种隐藏的心理倾向由每一次选择和行为悄悄塑造，界面不显示任何数字。序章结束时，游戏用一段“判词”告诉你：你一直在演的那个人，到底成了什么样。Four hidden tendencies are shaped by everything you do — and the interface never shows a number. At the end of the prologue the game tells you, in words, who you have been playing.

II

会说谎的内心声音Inner voices that lie

人格会“上浮”成脑中的声音：它们为你描述世界，染上自己的颜色，彼此争吵。想看见中立的事实，要停下来重新核对——信声音，还是信证据，由你决定。Your tendencies rise as voices in your head. They describe the world in their own colours and argue with each other. To see the plain facts you must stop and re-examine — trust the voice, or the evidence?

III

有分量的选择与检定Choices with weight

d20 技能检定（自然 20 与自然 1），可调的游戏难度；被动检定会在你没察觉时插话；你甚至要用内心检定去对抗自己的声音。失败也有内容。d20 skill checks (natural 20 / natural 1) with adjustable difficulty. Passive checks interrupt when you least expect it, and you can resist your own voice with an inner check. Failure is never “nothing happens”.

IV

话题式对话Topic-based conversations

走近一个人，打开一张话题表：读过的变灰，带技能的话题标出难度，成功多听到一句，失败只得到表层回答。Walk up to someone and open a topic list: read topics grey out, skill-gated topics show their difficulty, success earns an extra line, failure a polite brush-off.

V

会被修正的线索板A revisable clue board

白纸片是拿到的事实，黄纸片是你此刻的理解——它们可以被之后的证据推翻。序章之后，这套系统会长成完整的刑侦玩法。White slips are facts; yellow slips are your current theories — which later evidence can overturn. After the prologue this grows into full investigation gameplay.

VI

固定镜头的手绘世界A painted, fixed-camera world

写实比例的维多利亚时期室内，固定视角，由 3D 空间承载手绘质感；雨、雾与清晨到夜晚的光线实时变化。（美术制作中）Realistically proportioned Victorian interiors seen from fixed cameras, with a hand-painted look carried by real 3D space; rain, fog and a dawn-to-night light cycle in real time. (Art in production.)

我们如何制作How we build

先写好故事，再让系统可靠地跑起来。The story first; the systems built to carry it reliably.

  • 引擎：Godot 4（GDScript），目标平台 macOS。Engine: Godot 4 (GDScript), targeting macOS.
  • 剧本是唯一的真源：写在笔记里的文字被编译成游戏数据，改文字不需要改代码，旧存档仍能续玩。The script is the single source of truth: notes compile into game data, so rewriting text needs no code change and old saves keep working.
  • 模块化架构：每个系统只做一件事，互相不直接调用，只通过请求与通知协作。Modular architecture: each system does one thing and never calls another directly — only requests and notifications.
  • 自动化质量保障：330 项自动检查，以及一个会遍历每条分支、保证每个节点都能走到的试玩机器人。Automated quality: 330 automated checks and a playtest bot that walks every branch and verifies every node is reachable.
  • 工程协作：使用 Claude Code 作为工程搭档，把想法落成可维护的代码。Engineering partner: Claude Code, used to turn ideas into maintainable code.
  • 美术方向：手绘质感 + 固定镜头 3D，参考经典叙事 CRPG 的对话密度。Art direction: a painted look on fixed-camera 3D, with the dialogue density of classic narrative CRPGs.
